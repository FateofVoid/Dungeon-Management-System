# Global Lustria lore boundary

This project uses only setting-wide material from the supplied *Nexus of Lustrous Realms* source.

Lustria is an artificial nexus realm attributed to the Architect. Dungeon-controlled portals connect it to many homeworlds. Its enormous, shifting geography contains magical ecosystems, ancient ruins, independent peoples, city-states, nomadic cultures, merchants, adventurers, and competing powers. The Dominion of Lustria is the largest political and military authority and attempts to regulate dungeon expansion.

Dungeon thrones act as enduring cores. A throne selects and binds a master, stabilizes interdimensional portals, channels Lustrian power, and evolves with its bonded dungeon. Dungeons grow by unlocking facilities, population functions, defenses, and greater influence. Their resources and expansion bring them into conflict or cooperation with native peoples, merchants, adventurers, rival masters, and the Dominion.

Lustria supports broadly applicable resource-site lore used by the management system:

- Resonance Crystals guide magical and physical evolution.
- Mana-bearing flora naturally produces or concentrates magical energy.
- Ancient Architect-era ruins contain energized materials and artifacts.

The global lore intentionally excludes Eryndral, its nations and anomalies, Fate Veylark, Velis Reverie, Orphan, specific protagonists, and all story-specific dungeon descriptions. A scenario may define a Homeworld and personal story separately without adding those details to Lustria's global card.
