# Global Lustria lore boundary

The global Story Card registry contains only setting-wide Lustria foundations and universal dungeon rules. It provides shared context that remains valid regardless of the current Thronebound, Homeworld, dungeon theme, or scenario.

The supplied lore document is source material, not runtime instruction. Its setting-wide claims are compacted and reconciled with the DMS domain model before inclusion.

## Included foundations

The managed registry contains 110 narrowly triggered cards. Every entry is limited to 420 characters so the AI loads only the immediate fact it needs. Related cards link through distinctive terminology rather than repeating definitions.

The registry is divided into small concept families:

- **Core setting:** Lustria, the Architect, Architect ruins, peoples, crossworld culture, Dominion, thrones, the Thronebound bond, Themes, dungeon growth, magic, enchanted craft, evolution, ecosystems, resource Sites, trade, native interests, expansion conflict, rival dungeons, dungeon politics, Adventurer Guilds, the Veil, and the Verdant Accord.
- **Dungeon classification:** one Dungeon Tier overview, one Administrator Capacity card, and an individual card for every Tier from 0 through 10.
- **Rank classification:** one Rank overview, separate Administrator Rank and Level cards, and an individual card for F, E, D, C, B, A, S, SS, and SSS.
- **Grade classification:** one Grade overview, individual Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, and Mastery cards, plus separate overview, Unique, Apex, and Growth cards for special Grades.
- **Character development:** separate Evolution Tier, Class, Class Tier, Skill, Trait, Administrator, Linked Servant, Core Bond, population, and cohort cards.
- **Resources and commerce:** a Resource Role overview; individual Construction, Sustenance, Development, and Energy cards; and separate Lustrian Mark and trade cards.
- **Travel:** separate Gate Authority, Portal Anchor, Portal Gate development, and World Gate Array cards.
- **Dominion control:** separate Dungeon law, detection, Signature, and Signature suppression cards.
- **Geography:** separate geographic hierarchy, Sector, Resonance Core, persistent discovery, and resource-ecology cards.
- **Guild operations:** separate Guild institution, Quest Rank, Quest Category overview, Bounty, and Wild Dungeon cards, plus individual cards for all twelve standard Guild quest categories.
- **Dungeon conflict:** separate rivalry, Submission, Vassalization, Core Consumption, Legacy, Reformation, and general outcome cards.
- **Foundational principles:** separate Resonance and Dungeon Signature cards, with Architect mysteries left unresolved.

## Excluded material

The registry does not include:

- Aetheria, its Strata, MASS, Infinium, Genetic Essence, or any other Aetheria-specific setting or progression concept;
- Eryndral, its nations, technology, history, anomalies, or politics;
- Fate Veylark, Orphan, or any other scenario protagonist;
- Velis Reverie or another named player/scenario dungeon;
- character-specific abilities, equipment, relationships, backstory, or power claims;
- the Seven Sins dungeon history and named Demon Kings;
- speculative legends that are not necessary to the foundational setting;
- scenario outcomes presented as universal dungeon rules.

A scenario may define any of these subjects in its own cards. Those cards remain separate from the managed global registry.

## Canon reconciliation

The source describes thrones as effectively indestructible but also discusses a throne's destruction and reset. The global cards preserve the compatible foundation: a defeated or masterless dungeon can enter dormancy, rebuild, and reconnect because the throne's underlying function endures. They do not assert that ordinary forces can permanently destroy a throne.

The source also describes special servant links, divine dungeons, and sin-aligned growth. These are optional dungeon variants rather than universal requirements, so only the general principle—that an inherited theme may influence growth without dictating morality—is retained.

The expanded recommendations introduce several pieces of explicit DMS canon not established by the original source: the 0–10 Tier interpretation, major-link capacity, Lustrian Marks, Resource Grades, Sector Resonance Cores, Dungeon Signatures, and Resonance terminology. These additions standardize how Lustrian institutions classify radically different dungeons without defining what any player's dungeon must look like.

Tier 4 is treated as the Dominion's usual unsanctioned political ceiling, not a physical restriction or a law enforced identically everywhere. Resonance-Core origins, Wild Dungeon origins, core-consumption reformation, the Architect's purpose, and the ultimate meaning of Apotheosis remain unresolved.
