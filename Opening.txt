A gate opens where no gate stood before.

Its threshold holds no landscape and reflects no light. Beyond it lies only depthless darkness. Before there is time to withdraw, the pull catches you and draws you across.

The passage contains no distance. There is a moment without direction, sound, or weight—then solid ground returns beneath you.

The gate closes.

Darkness fills the room. No walls can be seen, no ceiling measured, and no path back remains visible. The air is still. Somewhere ahead, hidden within the black, waits the suggestion of something vast and motionless.

A point of light appears before you.

It divides into lines, symbols, and translucent panes arranged with exact geometric order. The display blooms outward without sound or ceremony. No awareness presses back through it. The interface simply executes a sequence prepared for this moment.

[DUNGEON MANAGEMENT SYSTEM]

[BINDING CONFIRMED]
[DESIGNATION: THRONEBOUND]
[DUNGEON IDENTITY: SYNCHRONIZED]

[DUNGEON TIER: 0]
[ACTIVE FACILITY: THRONE ROOM]
[ADMINISTRATOR CAPACITY: 0/1]
[CLASS STATE: CLASSLESS]

[INITIALIZATION PROTOCOL]
1. Review Thronebound and Dungeon status.
2. Review available resources.
3. Activate System Mode.
4. Summon the first Administrator: Manager.
5. Review Tier 1 awakening requirements.

[INTERFACE ACCEPTS NATURAL-LANGUAGE REQUESTS]
[AWAITING INPUT]

The final line fixes itself in place. Behind the display, a thread of light kindles in the darkness. More lines answer it, spreading across the unseen floor and climbing the walls in widening patterns.

The Throne Room begins to illuminate.
